Shadowmate Guide

Watch Netflix with subtitles in two languages and study one line at a time.

Getting started

  1. Play anything on Netflix and a panel appears at the bottom of the screen. The top line is the original (the language you're learning, e.g. English, Japanese, Spanish), and the bottom line is the translation (e.g. your language).
  2. To change languages, click the settings icon on the right of the panel and pick the original subtitles and translation subtitles. Your choice is remembered.
  3. Pin Shadowmate from the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar to open your saved items and this guide in one click.

A way to study

1. Watch once, relaxed

Don't hide anything at first. Follow the story with the translation. A whole episode is fine, and so is one short scene.

2. Hide and listen

Hide the original with W and turn on auto-pause with Q. Now the video stops at the end of every line. Listen with your ears only and work out what was said.

3. Didn't catch it? Again

Replay the line with S. If it's too fast, hear it at 0.75× with R. If pressing every time is tedious, turn on line repeat (2, 3 or 5 times) with T.

4. Check

Hover over the original line to see if you heard it right. Click a word you don't know, or drag over a phrase, to see its meaning, then save it with the star in the popup.

5. Say it out loud

Turn off hiding with W, read the original and say it out loud with the same speed and intonation. Replay with S and repeat two or three times. Once it feels easy, hide it again with W and try without looking. Record with B to compare your voice with the native speaker right away.

6. Next line

With the original hidden again, move on with D or the space bar and repeat from step 2. When you finish a scene, turn off auto-pause and watch the scene again from the start to see how much you can hear.

For a harder challenge, hide the translation too with E. You can export saved words and lines to Anki from your saved items to review them. Keys shown are the defaults.

Panel buttons and shortcuts

Previous line

Go to the start of the previous line.

A
Replay line

Hear the current line again from the start.

S
0.75×
Slow replay

Hear the current line again from the start at 0.75× speed. When the line ends, the speed goes back to what it was (including a speed set by a speed-control extension). Use it for lines that were too fast to catch.

R
Next line

Go to the start of the next line.

D
Auto-pause

When on, the video stops at the end of every line. Say it yourself, then continue with the space bar or replay with S.

Q
Repeat
Line repeat

Each press cycles Off → 2 → 3 → 5 times. When on, every line plays that many times, then moves on to the next (with auto-pause on, it stops after the last play). The button shows which play you're on, like "Rpt 2/3".

T
Copy line

Copy the current original line to the clipboard as one line. Handy for pasting into a translator or your notes.

C
Record & compare

Press it and the video pauses and recording starts (the button blinks red). Say the line, press again, and you hear the native line, then your recording. The first time, Chrome asks to let www.netflix.com use your microphone. The permission is stored for the Netflix site, so "Allow this time" or "Allow while visiting the site" is the safer choice.

B
Compare
Compare again

Hear the native line, then your recording, again for the line you just recorded. The button only shows on a line you recorded.

N
Hide original

Blur the original subtitles. Listen first, then hover over the line to check what you heard.

W
Hide translation

Blur the translation subtitles. Guess the meaning from the original, then hover to check. It's hidden in the subtitle list too.

E
Save line

Save the current line with its translation to your saved items. A filled star means it's saved; press again to remove it.

Subtitle list

Show all subtitles on the right. The current line is highlighted; click any line to jump to that scene.

Settings

Change the original and translation languages, hiding, compact view and shortcuts.

Timing bar

Each bar under the panel is one line of dialogue. The center mark is where you are now; click a bar to go to that line.

Press H to turn the whole panel off and go back to Netflix's own subtitles. While it's off, other keys go straight to Netflix; press H again to turn it back on. The keys above are the defaults. They follow the physical key position, so they work with any keyboard layout or input language.

Looking up words and phrases

One word

Click a word in the original subtitles to see its English definition (from Wiktionary) and a link to a dictionary in your language. The video stays paused meanwhile. Inflected forms with no entry of their own, like cities, are looked up by their base form, like city.

Idioms and phrases

Drag across several words to look up the whole expression. For example, drag beat me to the punch and it finds the dictionary form, beat someone to the punch.

Save

Press the star in the popup to save the word or phrase with its meaning, the original line and the translation.

Close

Press Esc, click outside, or move to the next line.

For now, word lookup only works when the original subtitles are English. Repeat, slow replay, hiding and record & compare work the same for every language.

Saved items and export

Open saved items

Click the Shadowmate icon in the toolbar to see your saved words and lines, newest first. Hover over an item and click × to delete it.

CSV

Download a file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.

Anki

In Anki, choose File → Import and pick the downloaded .txt file. The front (word or line), back (meaning and translation) and tags are set up automatically.

Saved items stay in Chrome, so signing out of Netflix doesn't delete them. Removing the extension does, so export them first.

Moving and resizing the panel

Compact view

Turn on Compact view in settings to show just the two subtitle lines on a dark background sized to the text (like Apple TV subtitles), with no buttons. Hover over the subtitles to bring back the buttons and timing bar. Shortcuts keep working while they're hidden.

Move

Drag an empty spot on the panel to move it anywhere. Double-click an empty spot to put it back.

Size

Drag the panel's top-right corner. Drag up or right to make it bigger, down or left to make it smaller. Double-click the corner to reset the size.

Compact view, position and size are remembered.

Changing shortcuts

  1. Click the settings icon on the panel.
  2. Click the key button next to the action you want to change, then press the new key. It's saved right away.
  3. If you pick a key another action uses, the two actions swap keys. Esc cancels, and Default restores the original keys.

Good to know

Netflix's own subtitles are hidden while the panel is showing.

The panel hides during ads, and the subtitle timing syncs up again when the ad ends. Skipping ads quickly with a speed-control extension is fine.

This extension takes over Netflix's S (skip intro) shortcut. If you don't want that, change the shortcut.

Turn off other subtitle extensions such as Language Reactor. They use the same keys and overlap on screen.

Your recorded voice stays only in this tab's memory for a moment and is cleared when you record again or switch to another title. It's never saved or sent anywhere, and the microphone turns off as soon as recording ends (up to 30 seconds at a time). While recording, the red mic button stays visible even in compact view.

Troubleshooting

The panel doesn't appear

Reload the Netflix tab. If you just updated the extension, first click Shadowmate's reload button in chrome://extensions.

It says "Loading … subtitles…"

Netflix hasn't sent subtitles in that language yet. Wait a few seconds and they'll appear.

It says to turn on … subtitles once in the Netflix subtitle menu

Pick that language once in the subtitle menu at the bottom of the Netflix player. It then shows up in the panel right away.

It says this title has no subtitles in the chosen language

In settings, pick another original language that this title has.

It says the microphone can't be used

Microphone access is blocked. Click the icon at the left of the address bar, allow the microphone for www.netflix.com, and try again.

It says the dictionary couldn't be loaded

Wiktionary blocks you briefly if you look up too much in a short time. Wait a bit and try again.
