Shadowmate Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

In short

Shadowmate does not collect personal information. There is no server, no analytics and no ads.

Stored only in your browser

  • Words and lines you save with ☆ (meaning, original line, translation, title name and playback time) are stored only in Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • Settings such as subtitle languages, shortcuts and panel position and size are stored only in the Netflix page's browser storage (localStorage).
  • This information is never sent to anyone, including the developer. Removing the extension also deletes your saved words.

Requests that leave your browser

  • Word lookup: When you click a word in the subtitles or drag over a phrase, that word (or phrase) is sent to Wiktionary (en.wiktionary.org) to get its meaning. The Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy applies.
  • Translated definitions: Chrome’s built-in Translator turns definitions into your translation subtitles’ language on this computer. Words and definitions are not sent anywhere. The first time, Chrome downloads that language’s translation model from Google.
  • Dictionary link: The popup's dictionary link (Naver, Weblio, Cambridge Dictionary or Google Translate, depending on your browser language) opens in a new tab only when you click it.
  • Netflix subtitles: Subtitle files Netflix has already provided are read from Netflix's servers and shown on screen.

Microphone (record & compare)

  • The microphone is used only when you press the record button (B). The first time, Chrome asks to let www.netflix.com use your microphone.
  • Your recorded voice stays only in that tab's memory and is cleared when you record again or switch to another title. It is never saved to a file or sent anywhere.
  • The microphone turns off as soon as recording ends. Each recording is up to 30 seconds.

Permissions

  • storage: Used to keep your saved words and lines in your browser.
  • Runs on www.netflix.com: Used to show the dual subtitle panel on Netflix. It does not run on any other site.

Contact

For questions, please open a GitHub issue. If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
